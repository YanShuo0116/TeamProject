
Unit 1: Greetings
Hello! How are you?
I'm fine, thank you.
Good morning. / Good afternoon. / Good evening.
Goodbye! See you tomorrow!

Unit 2: Introductions
My name is Amy. I am ten years old.
This is my friend, Tom.
Nice to meet you!
Where are you from? I’m from Taiwan.

Unit 3: Numbers and Colors
One, two, three, four, five.
Red, blue, green, yellow, pink.

Unit 4: Grammar Basics
The simple present tense is used to talk about things that happen every day.
Example: I go to school. She eats breakfast.

Be verbs: am, is, are.
I am a student. She is happy. They are friends.

Unit 5: Asking Questions
What is this? This is a pencil.
Where is the book? It is on the desk.
Do you like apples? Yes, I do.

Unit 6: Classroom Instructions
Open your book. Close the door. Raise your hand.
Listen carefully. Please be quiet.
